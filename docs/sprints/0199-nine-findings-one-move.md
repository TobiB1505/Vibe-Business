# Sprint 0199 — Nine findings, one Move

**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Decision:** module three is a narrowing — the candidates Vibe set aside, receding above the one it picked.

## What was asked

*"Modul 3 einmal lass du dir etwas einfallen was passen würde."*

## The shape had to be the argument, and a third one

Every module block on this page makes its point as a picture before anybody
reads a word, and each has to do it differently or a long scroll becomes one
rhythm. Step one is **two tiles** — a thing and an explanation. Step two is a
**staircase** — nine of something, walked. Step three is a **narrowing**.

That is what *prioritized* means, and it is the whole difference between Vibe
and a report. A report hands a founder nine findings and calls the ranking their
job. This block shows the ranking happening.

## Why the ones it did not pick are on screen

A single card would be a claim with nothing behind it. The three above it are
the findings Vibe set aside, each carrying the reason it ranks lower — *worth
fixing, but it changes nothing on its own*; *matters after people arrive and
pay*; *cheaper to fix once the offer is decided*. Without them, "one prioritized
move" reads as a product that found exactly one problem.

They recede rather than shrink. Shrinking would read as a ranking **among
themselves**, and the order Vibe puts those three in below the winner is not
something it publishes.

## The sentence that is also the sale

A Move is a **proposal**. Rule 54 says model output is an opinion and never
authority, so the block says it in the words a founder cares about: nothing
starts, no branch is touched, nothing is spent until they say so. The strongest
claim this page can make is the one the architecture already enforces.

## The fade was 4.10:1

The receding stack is the block's own argument for *these are sentences, not
decoration* — and at the first opacity ramp, 0.72 down to 0.40, the third row
rendered at `105,116,114` on an `8,10,12` ground. **4.10:1**, under AA for body
text, on a sentence the docblock two inches above it calls content.

Measured off the rendered page rather than reasoned about, and fixed by
measurement: 0.82 down to 0.60 puts the three rows at 10.76, 8.81 and 6.83 to
one. The guard asserts the effective opacity, which is what the pixels followed,
with a floor *and* a requirement that the recession still exists — a flat stack
at full strength fails it too.

## Moved, not copied

The winning card is `LandingFlow`'s *Prioritize* tab, and the flow is four tabs
now. Same dissolution the Product Scan started: a tab bar asks the reader to
stop and choose inside a page whose whole shape is a scroll, so the six steps
come out one at a time and their assertions move to the blocks that own them.

It is the product's own `FindingCard` on `variant="priority"`, so the landing
page cannot show a shape the app cannot produce — a severity, a confidence that
says what *kind* of confidence it is, and a citation naming where the
observation came from.

## Three guards, each broken to prove it

What it set aside, with the reason for each, and exactly one winner · every
set-aside finding above the readable floor, and still receding · a Move is a
proposal, nothing runs until you say.

Mutations: the opacity ramp put back under the floor, the recession flattened to
full strength, and the proposal sentence rewritten to claim Vibe starts work on
its own. All caught.
